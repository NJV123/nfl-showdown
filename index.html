🏆 Showdown — build your team

Two players take turns drafting 22 starters (11 offense, 11 defense) under a salary cap. Every player is rated out of 10 (his cost). A rare few earn an 11 for huge 2025 numbers: 1,600+ rushing or receiving yards, a QB with 4,500+ passing yards, or 15+ sacks, 6+ interceptions or 150+ tackles on defense. Then play head-to-head on one screen.

VS

A starting kicker is included for every team. Each player has six position-specific ratings (their overall is calculated from them) (for example Man/Zone/Press for corners, Break Tackle/Elusiveness for running backs, Pass/Run Block for linemen), generated from the overall rating with a per-player spread, not official data. Rosters: all 32 teams loaded, each with all 11 offensive + 11 defensive starters plus one backup per depth-chart spot (second string) and two extra reserves at every position (third string, injured-reserve and practice-squad players; ratings for deep reserves are estimates in the 55-68 range unless Madden-listed) (Ourlads depth charts, Oct 13-14 2026). OVR = 75% Madden NFL 27 rating + 25% my 2025-stats-based rating (players without a Madden listing keep the stats-based rating). Backups use their Madden rating when listed, otherwise an estimate of 60-70. Records are stored in this browser (localStorage). Export your data regularly for backup.
